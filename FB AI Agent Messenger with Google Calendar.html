Section 01 // Overview

Executive Summary

💡 Automating Staycation Bookings

Staycation properties, such as typical vacation rental units, generate substantial customer interest through Facebook Business Pages. Prospective guests continuously message late into the evening asking about unit capacity, wave pool access rules, specific date availability, and immediate reservations.

Prior to this system, property managers were burdened with around-the-clock manual monitoring. When guests reached out after hours (such as 10:00 PM to 2:00 AM), delays in answering availability questions frequently led to abandoned bookings and lost direct revenue. Traditional rigid chatbots failed because they could not check live calendar availability or book real slots dynamically.

This project demonstrates an autonomous, tool-enabled AI Customer Service & Booking Agent inside n8n (built as a proven mock testing sample). By equipping the Google Gemini Chat Model with a Knowledge Base retrieval tool and Google Calendar read/write tools, the bot independently answers amenities questions, verifies live room availability, and seals confirmed reservation dates directly on Google Calendar in seconds.

Section 02 // Problem Statement

The Core Challenge: Friction & Lost Revenue

Before the deployment of this autonomous tool-enabled workflow, property operations suffered from critical friction points that cost revenue daily.

🌙

01. Late-Night Lead Abandonment (10 PM – 2 AM)

Over 40% of staycation booking inquiries occur late at night when property managers are asleep. Unanswered Messenger inquiries resulted in travelers immediately booking competing units.

😩

02. Repetitive FAQ Answering Fatigue

Staff spent hours every week repeatedly typing out the same policies regarding wave pool operating schedules, security deposits, pet policies, and parking rates.

🤖

03. Inability of Traditional Chatbots

Standard keyword-based bots could only output rigid text trees. They lacked the intelligence to check live calendar availability or understand multi-variable queries.

⚠️

04. Double-Booking & Scheduling Risk

Manually transferring guest details between Messenger chats and Google Calendar created human errors, accidental date collisions, and missed reservation logging.

⏳

05. Meta Webhook Verification Collisions

Facebook's Messenger API requires strict GET challenge verification and instant HTTP 200 responses to prevent duplicate deliveries. Simple scripts frequently dropped webhooks.

📉

06. High Human Overhead

Property hosts spent excessive manual hours qualifying casual inquiries, leaving minimal focus for high-value operations, on-site guest hospitality, and facility upgrades.

STAGE 01
Dual-Path Webhook Architecture

The entry Webhook listens for both GET and POST requests. When Facebook pings the endpoint to verify authentication, the upper If branch intercepts the challenge and immediately returns the required verification token.

STAGE 02
Knowledge & Live Calendar Tools

The Access Knowledge Base tool equips Gemini with up-to-date property rules. The Check Slots tool executes live queries against the Google Calendar API, verifying date availability in real time.

STAGE 03
Automated Event Creation

Once the guest confirms, the Create Booking Schedule tool creates the event directly on the host's calendar, blocking dates and attaching guest details autonomously before replying via Meta Graph API.
