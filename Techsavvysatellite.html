Introduction

Techsavvy Satellite — Cross-Platform RevOps Automation

Techsavvy Satellite needed to streamline its field service operations and bridge communication gaps between technicians, office staff, and customers during service dispatch calls.

Challenges

  • Manual Dispatch Overhead: Customers need to be kept informed about changes to technician schedules and service appointments. For example, unexpected issues during transit or bad weather at the service location can cause delays, cancellations, or rescheduling. Without timely communication, customers may be left unaware of these changes
  • Lack of Real-Time Communication Office teams had limited visibility into field technician activities and dispatch updates, making it difficult to stay informed about ongoing jobs and respond quickly to operational changes
  • Poor Task Visibility: Office teams lacked a centralized way to track technician assignments, monitor job progress, and identify pending tasks, making it difficult to manage ongoing service operations.

The Solution

Engineered a custom, 24/7 digital intake platform paired with an automated pipeline process to educate prospects and capture leads effortlessly:

  • Automated Customer Alerts Implement automated email notifications that trigger whenever a technician's status or schedule changes. This ensures customers are automatically informed about delays, cancellations, and updated appointment schedules without requiring manual follow-ups.
  • Real-Time Slack Notifications: Automated dispatch updates and technician activity alerts into dedicated Slack channels, keeping internal teams informed whenever there were changes in job statuses, assignments, or field operations
  • Automated Task Tracking Integrated technician job updates with Asana to automatically create tasks, assign job details, and update task statuses as technicians progressed through their assigned jobs in the field.

The Results

  • Eliminated Manual Admin Work: Customers receive timely updates about their appointments, reducing confusion, missed appointments, and unnecessary follow-ups. The team also spends less time manually sending notifications, improving communication, customer experience, and overall service coordination.
  • Improved Internal Communication: Gave office teams instant access to field updates, reduced the need for manual status checks, and improved coordination between dispatchers, office staff, and field technicians.
  • Streamlined Task Management: Provided office managers with a centralized, up-to-date view of technician assignments and job progress, reducing manual task creation, repetitive status updates, and coordination gaps.
