Introduction

Facebook Page AI Agent — Autonomous Messenger & Google Calendar Booking Engine

Staycation properties and local service businesses receive continuous customer inquiries on Facebook Messenger late into the night regarding amenities, rules, and reservation dates, leading to delayed responses, high drop-off rates, and lost revenue during peak after-hours traffic.

The Challenges

  • Late-Night Lead Abandonment (10 PM – 2 AM): Over 40% of booking inquiries occur after business hours when hosts are asleep, causing prospective guests to drop off or reserve with faster-responding competitors.
  • Repetitive FAQ Answering Fatigue: Support teams spent excessive hours manually typing repetitive answers about amenities, operating rules, security deposits, and check-in schedules.
  • Double-Booking & Scheduling Inefficiencies: Traditional keyword chatbots could not check real-time calendar availability, forcing manual back-and-forth date verification and causing accidental scheduling collisions.

The Solution

Engineered an intelligent, tool-augmented automation pipeline in n8n combining the Meta Graph API, Google Gemini 1.5, and Google Calendar APIs:

  • Dual-Path Webhook Verification: Listens live via Facebook Webhooks, handling both Meta GET challenge handshakes and real-time POST message events with instant zero-drop responsiveness.
  • Knowledge Base Document Retrieval: Equips the Google Gemini AI Agent with structured knowledge base tools to instantly provide accurate answers on pricing, policies, and property guidelines.
  • Real-Time Calendar Availability & Direct Booking: Empowers the AI agent with read/write Google Calendar API tools to inspect live availability slots and automatically create confirmed booking events in seconds.

The Results

  • Sub-15s Response Turnaround: Replaced hours of manual delay with instant conversational answers and reservations delivered within 15 seconds 24/7.
  • 100% After-Hours Inquiry Capture: Eliminated lost midnight opportunities by autonomously engaging, qualifying, and booking guests while hosts sleep.
  • Zero Double Bookings: Live API checks across master Google Calendars ensured seamless date locking and complete scheduling accuracy without human overhead.
